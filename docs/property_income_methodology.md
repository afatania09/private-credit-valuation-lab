# Commercial-property income and refinancing analysis

## Financial bridge
Potential gross rent × (1 - vacancy) = effective gross income. Less property operating expenses gives **net operating income (NOI)**. Less recurring capital expenditure gives a simplified **cash flow available for debt service (CFADS)** proxy. Loan interest and principal are not deducted in NOI.

**DSCR** = CFADS / scheduled annual debt service. **Interest coverage** = NOI / annual interest. Lenders' definitions may differ and may include reserves, taxes, fees, cash traps, stressed interest rates and specified covenant adjustments.

## Direct capitalization
Indicative property value = stabilised NOI / capitalization rate. This simplified model assumes a stabilised perpetuity and does not replace a term-and-reversion valuation, explicit property DCF, comparable evidence or professional RICS valuation.

## Refinance
Two separate underwriting constraints:
1. LTV capacity = indicative property value × maximum permitted LTV.
2. DSCR capacity = CFADS / (minimum required DSCR × annual debt constant).

The smaller is indicative refinance capacity. The annual debt constant is scheduled debt service per pound of proposed new debt, **not** necessarily the coupon or market yield. Transaction fees, debt maturities, lender conditions, covenant definitions and refinance execution risk are not modelled.

## Worked synthetic example
Potential rent £10m, vacancy 10%, operating expenses £2m and recurring capex £0.5m imply NOI £7m and CFADS £6.5m. £70m of debt at 6% with £1m annual principal has annual debt service £5.2m, so DSCR is 1.25×. A 7% capitalization rate implies indicative property value £100m. A maximum 65% LTV permits £65m; minimum DSCR 1.3× with debt constant 9% permits about £55.56m, the binding constraint. This indicates a £14.44m refinance gap versus £70m outstanding, **before fees and other considerations**.

## Model governance
No market evidence, lender-specific covenants, rent-roll inputs, lease expiries, tenant credit or appraisal assurance is included. The resulting numbers are screening sensitivities, not IFRS 13 fair values or IFRS 9 ECL.
